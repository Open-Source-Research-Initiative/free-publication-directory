# Free publication directory

This is an OSRI directory of research dissemination venues with a zero-cost author route. People direct Agentic-Powered-Research. Initial healthcare work is nonclinical; new physical laboratory experiments are outside the initial program.

- Read `research/CONTRACT.md` before adding or reviewing records. Follow assigned ownership and preserve other agents' files.
- Verify current material claims using official venue or publisher sources. A search result snippet is weaker evidence than the policy page. Record ambiguity instead of inventing a fee, metric, indexing claim, or acceptance rate.
- Submission fees, publication fees, mandatory membership, page charges, and conference registration are separate costs. Optional open access does not disqualify a genuinely free subscription route. Waivers and institutional agreements are conditional routes.
- Preprints, repositories, and blogs are separate from peer-reviewed publication. Citations and prestige are separate from acceptance probability. No guaranteed acceptance claims.
- Keep sources, dates, article-type conditions, metric years, and agent-review limitations with the data. Do not copy entire publisher pages.
- Do not create a remote repository, push, merge, deploy, contact a venue, or submit research without the human's authorization for that action.
